Tools and Access

The sourcing stack, who owns each tool, and how to get added.

Primary

Juicebox

Our sourcing platform for lead generation, email sequencing, and sourcing analytics.

DRI: Dom Terry. To be added as a user, contact Dominique Terry.

Sunsetting Jan 2027

Gem

Our prior one-stop shop for email sequencing, prospect data (CRM), sourcing dashboards and reports, and AI-powered lead generation. Gem Academy comes with the platform.

DRI: Dom Terry. To be added as a user, ask a member of the talent team for an invitation. The subscription ends in January 2027.

LinkedIn Recruiter

Used for general engagement, connection requests, InMail, talent mapping, and compensation research. Seats are limited.

To discuss a seat, message John Liebman, Senior Manager of Talent Acquisition, on Slack. There is a Loom tutorial: Using LinkedIn Recruiter.

Greenhouse

Our applicant tracking system and the database for active candidate information. Interviewers, recruiters, and hiring managers can view candidates and complete scorecards.

DRI: Lisa White, Recruiting Coordinator. To be added, ask a member of the talent team for an invitation.

Calendly

Scheduling, integrated with Greenhouse. Candidates already in Greenhouse are scheduled through Calendly, which creates the interview in Greenhouse automatically. Prospects not yet in Greenhouse are also scheduled through Calendly, but you add them to Greenhouse by hand.

Who adds new Calendly users: to be confirmed. Ask the talent team.

Claude

Claude Cowork plus the Sourcing Intelligence plugin automates strategy, outreach drafting, and research, and can run batch sourcing. See Claude Setup.

Slack

Slack groups such as Future of Higher Education (FOHE) or datatalks are good places to post jobs to specialized practitioners. Curated groups are in the Channel Directory in Notion.
